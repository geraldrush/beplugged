Photos You Can Hold

BePlugged Studio makes hardcover photobooks, diaries and gift boxes. The pictures on your phone are the ones nobody ever sees again — we lay them out, print them and bind them into a book that stays on a shelf instead of in a folder.

Case bound, like a proper book

The pages are bound into a block, then set into a rigid case made separately — the way a hardcover book has always been put together. Made to be opened again in ten years rather than to survive one evening of being passed around.

We do the layout

You send the photos. We arrange them, place the captions and names, and handle the pictures that came out too dark or too small to use as they are.

Nothing prints unapproved

You see a proof of every page before anything goes to print. Names, dates and spelling are yours to check, and changes at that stage cost nothing.

Design your own book

Prefer to lay it out yourself? Open the book designer, choose a size, drop your photos onto the pages and send it to us when it looks right. It saves as you go, so you can stop and come back.

Books For The Days That Matter

The same book, shaped around the occasion. Tell us which one you are marking and we will tell you what usually works for it.

Birthdays

A year, a decade, or a whole life so far. Milestone birthdays where the family has photos scattered across five phones are what these books are best at.

Weddings

The album your photographer's files deserve, plus the guest photos that never made the official set. Extra copies for parents are ordered at the same time.

Funerals and memorials

A memorial book for the family, and for the people who come to remember. These are usually needed quickly — tell us the date and we will say honestly whether we can meet it.

Parties and celebrations

Anniversaries, reunions, baby showers, farewells and year-end functions. One book for the host, or a short run for everyone who was there.

Graduations

The gown, the certificate, and the years of work behind them. Popular as a gift from parents, and as a class book for a school or a group finishing together.

Anything worth keeping

Family history, a first year, a trip, a team, a business. If the photos exist and the occasion matters to you, it can be a book.

Diaries And Journals

Hardcover diaries made the same way as the books, with your own cover on the front.

Dated diaries

Day or week to a page, printed for the year you need, with your name or your organisation's on the cover.

Undated journals

Lined, plain or dotted pages for people who would rather not have a diary that guilts them about January.

Branded diaries for teams

A run of diaries carrying your logo, for staff, clients or delegates. Sensible for year-end gifts, conferences and school leadership groups.

Gift Boxes

A book on its own is a gift. A book in a box is the one people photograph before they open it.

Book and box sets

Your photobook or diary presented in a rigid box made to fit it, so it arrives as something to unwrap rather than something to unwrap the courier bag of.

Occasion boxes

Put together for a birthday, a wedding party, a graduation or a thank you, with the printed pieces that go alongside the book.

Corporate gift boxes

Branded boxes for clients, staff or delegates, in a quantity we agree up front so you are not left with a cupboard of leftovers.

How An Order Works

Six steps, and you decide at the fourth one whether it goes to print.

1Tell us the occasion

What the book is for, roughly how many photos you have, and the date you need it in your hands. That is enough for us to quote.

2Send the photos

WhatsApp, email or a shared folder — whichever you already use. Send the originals rather than screenshots, and we will sort out the rest.

3The book gets designed

We choose the order, the layout and the crops, and fix what can be fixed in the images — or you do it yourself in the book designer and we work from that.

4You approve a proof

Every page, before printing. Check the spelling of names especially — once it is bound, a correction means a new book.

5We print and bind

Printing, cover, binding and the box if you ordered one. Extra copies are far cheaper ordered now than reprinted later.

6Collection or delivery

Collect from us in Pretoria, or we courier it. For funerals we work to the service date and tell you up front if it is too tight.

What A Book Costs

Every book is made to order, so there is no single price. Three things decide it, and you control all three.

Size and page count

A bigger book with more pages costs more. We will tell you how many pages your photos actually need before you commit to a number.

Cover and finish

The cover material and finishing are where a book moves from good to special. We quote the options rather than assume the expensive one.

How many copies

The first copy carries the design work. Copies two, three and ten are much cheaper — which is why we ask how many before we print, not after.
