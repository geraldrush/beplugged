What We Build

We design and build websites, applications and business systems for companies, schools and organisations — and we stay involved once they are live.

Websites

Business and brochure sites, and sites you can update yourself, built to load quickly and work properly on a phone.

Web Applications

Logins, dashboards, bookings and payments — software that runs in the browser and does real work.

Mobile Apps

Applications for phones and tablets, built alongside the systems that feed them.

Custom Business Systems

CRM, invoicing, admin, booking and school systems shaped around how you actually operate.

Ecommerce

Online stores with payment, stock and order handling that your team can run without us.

Databases and Reporting

Data designed so it can be trusted, and reports that answer the questions you actually ask.

Cloud, Security and Support

Building something is the first part. Keeping it running, secure and understood by your own team is the rest.

Cloud Hosting and Migration

Setup, migration and ongoing management across major cloud platforms, sized to what you need rather than what you might.

Security and Access

Website security, DNS, email routing, domain configuration and access management, reviewed rather than assumed.

Support and Maintenance

Ongoing technical support, updates and monitoring, so someone is responsible when something breaks.

Process Automation

Removing the manual steps between your systems, so work moves without being retyped.

Digital Transformation

Practical advice on what to build, what to buy, and what to leave alone.

Handover Training

Training your team to run what we build, so you are not dependent on us to change a page.
