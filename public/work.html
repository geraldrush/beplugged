What This Work Has In Common

Different industries, and mostly the same starting point: a business that already worked, with nothing online that carried its weight.

From nothing to found

Matria, PJ Properties and Afroscaff were built from scratch. All three now reach people who were never going to hear about them by word of mouth, which for a trades or construction business is most of the market.

Built to a brief, not a template

ACTS specified their platform request by request and we built to it. ProsDirectory and the Extra Classes app hold real users and real data — profiles, locations, subjects, progress — rather than a set of pages.

Not only South Africa

Manica Health Services is in Canada. ACTS is an international organisation and Murek delivers across Africa. Being in Pretoria has not turned out to be a limit on who we work with.
