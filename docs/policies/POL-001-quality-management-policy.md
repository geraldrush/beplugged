## 1. Purpose

To state how Beplugged Tech defines quality in the work it delivers, and the commitments it makes to achieve it consistently.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all client work — websites, web applications, and related services — and to everyone performing that work on behalf of Beplugged Tech, including contractors.

## 3. Policy statement

Beplugged Tech delivers work that does what the client agreed it would do, on the date agreed, at the price agreed. Where any of those three cannot be met, the client is told as soon as it is known rather than at the deadline.

Quality is not a final inspection step. It is the result of understanding requirements before quoting, agreeing scope in writing, and checking work against that scope before it is handed over.

## 4. Commitments

Beplugged Tech commits to:

1. **Understand before pricing.** No quote is issued against assumed requirements. Requirements are confirmed in writing with the client (SOP-CLI-001).
2. **Agree scope explicitly.** Every quote states what is included and what is excluded. Changes are priced and agreed before being built (SOP-FIN-002).
3. **Test before handover.** No work goes live without completing the launch checklist (SOP-DEL-001 §8).
4. **Communicate honestly.** Delays, blockers, and mistakes are raised early. A problem disclosed is a project issue; a problem concealed is a client relationship issue.
5. **Keep records.** Decisions, approvals, and changes are recorded so that what was agreed can be established later without relying on memory.
6. **Correct and learn.** Where something goes wrong, the immediate fix is applied and the cause recorded so the same failure is not repeated (§6).
7. **Protect client information.** Handled per the Information Security Policy (QMS-2026-POL-002).

## 5. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Sets this policy, provides the time and tools to meet it, and reviews performance |
| **Anyone performing client work** | Works to the applicable SOPs and raises issues rather than working around them |

## 6. Measuring and improving

Quality performance is reviewed at each management review (QMS-2026-MRV-001) against:

- Projects delivered by the agreed date
- Change requests arising from unclear original scope
- Defects found by the client after handover
- Client feedback (QMS-2026-FDB-001)
- Corrective actions raised, and whether they recurred

Where a measure moves the wrong way, a corrective action is raised rather than noted.
