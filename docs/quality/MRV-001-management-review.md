## 1. Purpose

To set a fixed agenda for reviewing how the business is actually performing, and to record the decisions made. Without a scheduled review, registers get filled in and never read.

## 2. Scope

Held quarterly. Attended by the owner, plus anyone accountable for an area under review. Expected to take 45–60 minutes.

## 5. Rules

1. **Hold it even when busy.** A skipped review becomes a skipped quarter, then a skipped year.
2. **Every action gets an owner and a date.** Otherwise it is a wish.
3. **Record decisions, not discussion.** The value is in what was decided.
4. **Carry unfinished actions forward** explicitly rather than letting them lapse.
